I'm Yuong. I work across graphic design and production, bringing creative ideas to life without losing sight of the details that make them work.

For nearly a decade, I've worked in educational publishing, visual identity, and production. I enjoy turning information-heavy projects into something clear, useful, and engaging.

I'm just as invested in how a project comes together as I am in the initial idea—from refining layouts and preparing files for print to checking quality, coordinating with vendors, and managing updates and reprints across a library of 100+ books.
